A short timeline of the sources cited in this catalogue.

  • 1965: Walkup counts T-tetromino rectangle tilings modulo 4 ().
  • 1966: Golomb's paper on tiling with polyominoes ().
  • 1969: Klarner studies packing rectangles with congruent polyominoes and the order (); Klarner and Göbel () and de Bruijn () treat boxes packed with bricks.
  • 1989: Golomb's paper on polyominoes which tile rectangles (), and Dahlke's orders 92 for the Y-hexomino and 76 for a heptomino (, ).
  • 1992: Stewart and Wormstein show that order 3 does not occur ().
  • 1997: Reid finds new rectifiable polyominoes and families that tile half-strips ().
  • 2005: Reid on Klarner systems and prime rectangles ().
  • 2019: Tulleken's book collects the theory and open problems, listing as Problem 52 ().
  • Dahlke's Gun Theorem classifies the width-2 polyominoes, leaving unresolved ().
